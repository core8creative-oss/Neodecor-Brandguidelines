ROOM 06 · GUEST ROOM

Imagery, real homes

Shot guideWhat to avoid

6.1 Photography

Real homes, real light

Every finished home is the best advertisement. Shoot each handover the same way so the feed reads like one portfolio.

SHOT GUIDE · WIDE
01

Wide and straight

Shoot the whole room from the doorway, camera at chest height, verticals straight.

  • Wide lens, no fish-eye
  • Lights on, curtains open, daylight in
SHOT GUIDE · DETAIL
02

Then the details

Handles, hinges, profile lights, inside a drawer. Quality lives in close-ups.

  • Open one drawer or shutter
  • Show finish texture: laminate, veneer, quartz
SHOT GUIDE · STYLED
03

Styled, not staged

A plant, a bowl, a book — signs of life. Remove packaging, tools and personal clutter.

  • Three props per frame at most
  • Clean every surface before shooting
SHOT GUIDE · DESIGN → REAL
3D render
Same angle, real
04

Match the render

Shoot the finished room from the same angle as its 3D design. The pair becomes your best post.

  • Save the render angle at sign-off
  • Ask the homeowner before posting their home
AVOIDStock photos presented as our work
AVOIDYellow night-time light with no daylight
AVOIDTilted, fish-eye or dark phone shots
AVOIDHalf-finished sites in portfolio posts
AVOIDHomeowners’ faces or family photos without consent

The website hero uses a stock photo. As soon as there are photos of the Shriram Liberty Square homes, replace it — real work builds trust faster.
